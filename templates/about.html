Our story

Built as a campus, not a building

New Heaven opened in 2015 with two classroom blocks and a dirt football field. The playing fields came before most of the current classrooms did — a sequencing choice the school has kept ever since.

Mission

To teach the full WAEC secondary curriculum to a high academic standard, while giving every student regular, structured time on a sports field, in a workshop, or in front of an audience — not as enrichment, but as timetabled coursework.

Vision

A school leaver who can defend an argument in an exam hall, hold a position on a football pitch, and finish something they started in a workshop or lab, without treating any of the three as the "extra" one.

How we're organised

Three phases across grades 6–11

Grades 6–7

Foundation years

Form tutors teach across most subjects, homework is set on a fixed weekly rhythm, and PE happens in the junior play yard rather than the senior ground.

Grades 7–9

Junior secondary

Subject specialists take over, science moves into the labs, and students choose their first elective — a language, a craft, or an instrument.

Grades 9–11

Senior secondary

Students select a WAEC subject combination, sit mock exams each term, and take on a leadership role — prefect, team captain, or club lead.
