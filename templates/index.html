What the campus is for

Four grounds, one gate

Everything sits inside the same boundary wall, so a free period can mean the library, the pitch or the lab — without leaving school property.

Academics

Three-block classroom wing

Homerooms, science and computer labs, and the library sit across three floors of the main building, arranged so subject wings don't cross corridors during the day.

Sport

Full-size pitch and track

A regulation football pitch with a surrounding running track and a fenced basketball court host PE classes by day and inter-house fixtures after 3pm.

Junior years

Dedicated play yard

Grades 6 and 7 have their own fenced play area with swings and slides, separated from the senior sports ground so younger students aren't competing for space.

Why families choose us
