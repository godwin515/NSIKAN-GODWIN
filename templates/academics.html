Curriculum

A WAEC curriculum, taught in fixed subject blocks

Every grade follows the same six-day teaching cycle: core subjects in the morning, electives and labs after lunch, and one afternoon a week set aside for sport and clubs.

Core subjects

Taught in every grade, 6 through 12

English Language

Composition, comprehension and literature, with a dedicated reading period each week.

Mathematics

Arithmetic through calculus, streamed by ability from Grade 9 onward.

Basic & Integrated Science

Splits into Biology, Chemistry and Physics from Grade 9.

Social Studies

Nigerian history, civics and geography, taught jointly through Grade 8.

Senior electives

Chosen at the start of Grade 10

Students pick a four-subject combination alongside the core, sat as their eventual WAEC papers.

How the week is structured

A six-day cycle, not a five-day week

  1. Days 1–2: core subjects

    Mathematics, English and science are taught in double periods each morning, in the same rooms in the same order across the cycle.

  2. Days 3–4: electives and labs

    Afternoons move to the science and computer labs, or to elective subject rooms for the senior tracks.

  3. Day 5: assessment and review

    Short tests, marked work returned, and form-tutor time to go over results before the weekend.

  4. Day 6: sport, clubs and workshops

    The full afternoon moves outdoors or into the workshop and studio spaces — see student life.
