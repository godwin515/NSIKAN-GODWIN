Timeline

How the process runs

  1. October: applications open

    Submit the form below along with the student's most recent two terms of school reports.

  2. November: entrance assessment

    A half-day written assessment in English and Mathematics, held on campus on a Saturday.

  3. Early December: family interview

    A short conversation with the student and a parent or guardian, with the relevant Head of Year.

  4. Mid-December: offers issued

    Offers are sent by email and post, with two weeks to accept and pay the confirmation deposit.

  5. January: induction day

    New students spend a morning meeting their form tutor and touring the campus before term starts.
